● STRATEGIC BUSINESS INSIGHTS

Business Insights

Actionable conclusions generated directly from the analyzed dairy database.

📈

Seasonal Demand & Sales Trend

Sales recorded clear demand surges during summer (March–May) and festive quarters (August–November). Summer creates high demand for fermented beverages (Buttermilk, Lassi, Curd), whereas festive quarters witness maximum Pure Ghee and Sweets procurement.

🏆

Value-Added Product Margin Leaders

Nandini Toned Milk and Buttermilk generated the highest sales volume among products, anchoring daily customer retention. Simultaneously, Nandini Pure Ghee (1L) drove the highest total revenue (₹1.38 Cr), demonstrating the vital role of value-added dairy processing.

📍

Regional Market Penetration

Certain locations consistently generated higher revenue than others. Bengaluru Metropolitan accounts for 44.58% of total turnover, followed by Mysuru (15.02%) and Hubballi (10.74%).

⚠️

Cold-Chain & Niche Optimization

Some niche products like Cassata Ice Cream and Kulfi recorded relatively lower sales volume and revenue in tier-2 locations, presenting opportunities for targeted promotional pricing and cold-chain upgrades.

📦

Statewide Distribution Reliability

Average delivery efficiency across authorized distributor networks stands at 98.2%. Returned quantities can be closely tracked to minimize logistics inefficiencies in summer months.
